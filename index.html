About this messaging program

Imogen is personal, non-commercial assistant software run by Dai Nguyen, a sole proprietor, on his own computer. It sends and receives text messages from the toll-free number (844) 430-2899.

  • Only one recipient: the number owner's own mobile phone. Imogen never texts anyone else.
  • What the messages are: replies to short commands the owner sends (for example STATUS or TASKS), plus occasional reminders and status alerts about his own to-do list and monitoring software he runs.
  • What they are not: no marketing or promotional messages, no third-party recipients, and no purchased, rented or shared contact lists.

How opt-in works (keyword: IMOGEN)

The only person who receives messages is the owner himself. He opts in by texting the keyword IMOGEN from his own mobile phone to (844) 430-2899, then confirming. Until opt-in is confirmed, Imogen sends no other messages to that number. Nobody else's number is ever added.

  1. Owner texts: IMOGEN to (844) 430-2899
  2. Imogen replies: “Imogen: Welcome aboard. You've opted in to Imogen personal assistant texts from Dai Nguyen. Msg frequency varies (typically a few per day). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Terms & privacy: https://biglittledai.github.io/Imogen/ Reply Y to confirm.”
  3. Owner texts: Y
  4. Imogen confirms: “Imogen: You're all set. Text ? for commands.”

Replying STOP at any point ends all messages; texting IMOGEN again restarts the opt-in.

Privacy policy

  • The only phone number Imogen stores is the owner's own, kept in a local configuration file on his computer.
  • Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent are never shared with, sold to, or rented to third parties or affiliates for any purpose, including marketing.
  • Message content is processed only to carry out the owner's own requests. It passes through the SMS provider (Twilio) as required to deliver texts, and is not used for any other purpose.
